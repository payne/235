# 9 PM Friday Technical Ragchew NET

Welcome to a weekly gathering of amateur radio operators on the W0JJK repeater in Omaha, Nebraska.

## When
**Fridays at 9:00 PM Central Time** *(Omaha, Nebraska local time)*

## Where
**[W0JJK 145.235 (-) MHz, PL 131.8](https://www.repeaterbook.com/repeaters/details.php?state_id=31&ID=54)**
- Frequency: 145.235 MHz
- Offset: Negative (-)
- CTCSS Tone: 131.8 Hz

## What
A traditional HAM radio **round robin ragchew** and **technical discussion NET**. Share what's new in your shack, discuss antenna projects, or just enjoy good conversation with fellow hams.

## Why
> **More Radio** creates **More Fun** attracts **More People** enables **More Radio**

## Who
**Anyone with a HAM radio license!** Technicians, Generals, and Extras welcome. If you can hit the repeater, we want to hear from you.

## How
Brought to you by **W0JJK** and a growing group of **Net Control Stations (NCS)**. Tune in Friday night and check in when net control calls for stations. We run a friendly, informal net — perfect for new hams and old timers alike.

## Join Us This Friday!
Program 145.235 into your radio, set your tone to 131.8, and we'll see you on the air!

**73 de 235 NET**
