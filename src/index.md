# 235 NET

## The Friday Night Ragchew

Welcome to the **235 NET** — a weekly gathering of amateur radio operators on the W0JJK repeater in Omaha, Nebraska.

---

## When

**Fridays at 9:00 PM Central Time**
*(Omaha, Nebraska local time)*

---

## Where

**[W0JJK 145.235 (-) MHz, PL 131.8](https://www.repeaterbook.com/repeaters/details.php?state_id=31&ID=54)**

- Frequency: 145.235 MHz
- Offset: Negative (-)
- CTCSS Tone: 131.8 Hz

---

## What

A traditional HAM radio **round robin ragchew** and **technical discussion NET**.

Whether you want to share what's new in your shack, discuss antenna projects, or just enjoy some good conversation with fellow hams — this is your net!

---

## Why

> **More Radio** creates **More Fun** attracts **More People** enables **More Radio**

It's a virtuous cycle. The more we get on the air, the more fun we have. The more fun we have, the more people want to join. And the more people join, the more radio we all get to enjoy!

---

## Who

**Anyone with a HAM radio license!**

Technicians, Generals, and Extras are all welcome. If you can hit the repeater, we want to hear from you.

---

## How

Brought to you by **W0JJK** and a growing group of **Net Control Stations (NCS)**.

Simply tune in on Friday night and check in when net control calls for stations. We run a friendly, informal net — perfect for new hams and old timers alike.

---

## Join Us This Friday!

Program 145.235 into your radio, set your tone to 131.8, and we'll see you on the air!

**73 de 235 NET**
